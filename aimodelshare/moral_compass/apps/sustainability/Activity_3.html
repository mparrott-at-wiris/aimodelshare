🧠 How Does an AI Model Learn?

It Learns from Examples

An AI model isn't programmed with answers. It's trained on thousands of past building records and learns to find the patterns that connect a building's features to its energy efficiency.

The Training Process

The AI trains by practicing over and over. Click the button to watch it learn:

During each cycle, the model adjusts its internal rules (called "weights") to get closer to the right answer. It learns how much each feature matters — like whether building age matters more than floor area.
