📊 Step 3: Define the Success Metric

Your AI needs a single score to compare a skyscraper to a small house. We call this Energy Usage Intensity (EUI).

Understanding EUI

EUI normalizes energy consumption by building size, allowing fair comparisons.

(Electricity + Natural Gas) ÷ Floor Area = Site EUI
Electricity
kWh/year
Natural Gas
kBTU/year
Floor Area
sqft

📌 EXAMPLE:

  • Low EUI (50 kBTU/sqft): Efficient building, good insulation, modern HVAC
  • High EUI (200 kBTU/sqft): Inefficient building, priority for retrofit
